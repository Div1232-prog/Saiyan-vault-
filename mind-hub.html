NEXUS / SUPPORT

Help Center

Quick answers for common browsing and account questions.

How do I download a wallpaper?

Tap the Download 4K control on a wallpaper card, or open its preview and use the download control there.

Are wallpapers free?

The collection is offered for personal, non-commercial wallpaper use. Downloading does not transfer copyright or commercial rights.

How do I change themes?

Use the theme selector in the header and choose one of the four visual themes. Your choice is saved in this browser.

How do global reactions work?

Firebase Anonymous Authentication gives your browser a guest UID. Firestore accepts one reaction for each UID and wallpaper; shared counts update automatically.

What is NEXUS Originals?

It is the collection label for original character artwork created for the NEXUS collection. Third-party fan art and copyrighted characters are not represented as NEXUS-owned original IP.

What if an image does not load?

Check your connection and retry. If it remains broken, use the existing Feedback / Bug Report link in the footer and include the wallpaper title.

Why are reactions not updating?

Check your connection, Firebase Anonymous Authentication, Firestore deployment, and the published Security Rules. If Firebase is unavailable, reaction controls show an error state instead of claiming a vote succeeded.

Can I sync favourites?

Open Account Sync and sign in with email/password or Google. Local favourites remain available on this device.
