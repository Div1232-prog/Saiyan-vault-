Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXUS Wallpapers (https://saiyan-vault.pages.dev), an independently managed website, handles information when you browse the website, save wallpapers, use its optional account feature, or contact the site owner.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The website uses your browser's local storage to provide basic features. In the current website code, this includes:

  • Saved favourites: wallpaper IDs saved on your current browser/device.
  • Recently viewed wallpapers: wallpaper IDs used to display your recent items on that browser/device.
  • Policy notice preference: a small preference record so the first-visit Terms & Privacy notice does not keep appearing after you accept it.
  • Accent theme preference: the selected Cyberpunk, Crimson Gothic, or Matrix colour theme.
  • Global reactions: a Firebase anonymous user ID, wallpaper ID, selected reaction type, and aggregate reaction counters are processed/stored by Cloud Firestore to enforce one vote per authenticated UID per wallpaper and show shared counts.
  • Mystery Wallpaper attempts: the current local-calendar-day attempt count and selected card needed to provide the daily scratch-card feature.

This browser storage is used to remember site preferences. It is not, by itself, a website account and does not automatically sync between devices. You can remove these records by clearing this website's browser storage, although doing so will also remove locally saved favourites and recent items.

Cookies: The current website code does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses browser local storage for the features described above. If optional account synchronization is configured and enabled in a future deployment, the relevant third-party authentication/storage technology may also be used as described below.

2. Optional Account & Favourites Synchronization

NEXUS uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for global wallpaper reactions and optional account-based favourites sync. Visitors may receive an anonymous Firebase user ID so the site can enforce one reaction per authenticated UID per wallpaper without asking for an account. If you choose email/password or Google sign-in, Firebase may also process your email address and authentication information.

Cloud Firestore stores reaction documents (wallpaper identifiers, reaction counts, and one vote record per Firebase UID) and, when you use account sync, your account's saved wallpaper IDs and an update timestamp. Password authentication is handled by Firebase; the website owner does not receive or store your account password. Do not reuse a password from another service.

Firebase is a third-party service operated by Google. Its processing is subject to Google's/Firebase's applicable terms and privacy information. The website loads Firebase modules only when the account feature is opened and a valid configuration is present.

3. Information You Send to Us

If you contact the website owner by email, the owner will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name, message, and any files or URLs you provide. This information is used to respond to your enquiry, handle copyright reports, or address website-related requests. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

4. Hosting, Technical Data & External Links

The website is delivered through a third-party hosting platform. The hosting, network, and security providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, and request timestamps to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website. NEXUS Wallpapers does not operate those infrastructure systems directly.

The website uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for guest identity, global reactions, and optional account favourites sync. The Google sign-in provider is available when enabled in Firebase. The site does not initialize Google Analytics in this build. External services such as Pinterest are subject to their own terms and privacy notices.

The supplied HTML currently contains no Google Analytics or advertising tag/script. If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking tools are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before those tools are activated.

5. How We Use Information

Information handled by the website is used only for purposes connected with operating its features, such as remembering favourites and recent items, providing optional account synchronization if enabled, responding to emails, reviewing copyright notices, and maintaining website security and functionality.

NEXUS Wallpapers does not sell personal information. The site is independently managed and does not maintain a customer advertising database.

6. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local favourites and recently viewed items remain in your browser until you remove them or clear the website's browser storage.
  • Emails remain in the website owner's email account for as long as reasonably needed to respond, keep relevant records, or handle a legal request.
  • If Firebase synchronization is enabled, account data is retained by the configured Firebase project until it is deleted through the account/service controls or by the project owner, subject to applicable provider retention practices.

You can stop using the site, clear its local storage, avoid opening third-party links, or contact us with a privacy request. Clearing browser storage does not delete any data already stored by a third-party service.

NEXUS Wallpapers is a general-audience wallpaper website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Visitors should not submit unnecessary personal information. Parents or guardians who believe a child has sent personal information to the site may contact us so we can review the request.
