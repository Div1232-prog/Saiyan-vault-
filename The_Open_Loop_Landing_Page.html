THE IDEA BEHIND THE LOOP

THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE OPEN LOOP

The Zeigarnik Effect is associated with the observation that unfinished tasks can sometimes remain more accessible in memory than completed ones. It is a useful idea to explore—not a universal rule.

01

THE OPEN LOOP

Unfinished tasks may continue to occupy our attention or return to mind.

02

THE FRICTION

Getting started can feel difficult, even when the next action is manageable.

03

THE FIRST STEP

A small, concrete starting action can make beginning feel more approachable.

Important: the Zeigarnik Effect does not mean the brain never forgets unfinished tasks, and it is not a guaranteed cure for procrastination. Individual experiences vary.
